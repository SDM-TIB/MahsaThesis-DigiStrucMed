06 / EVALUATE, SELECT, REPEAT

Score, rank, and repeat the search

  1. Generate: propose candidate editing prompts.
  2. Evaluate: run them on validation examples and score each one.
  3. Select: keep the highest-scoring prompts.
  4. Repeat: use them as parents for the next generation.
  5. Test: compare the starting and selected prompts on held-out requests.

The best prompt is whichever one wins under the scoring function.

10 / HOW A REQUEST FLOWS

Draft first, then GAAPO-selected editing

  1. User: enters audience, language, duration, and topic.
  2. Medical model: the GuidelineKG adapter writes the draft.
  3. GAAPO artifact: the app loads the saved best prompt.
  4. Editing model: gets the request, the draft, and that prompt.
  5. Output: a plain-text Animaker script, with the draft kept alongside it.

Literature upload is optional; without it, the editor still improves presentation and flags gaps instead of inventing sources.

APPENDIX / REPRODUCIBILITY

Exact saved GAAPO prompt

You are a medical educational storyboard generator. Convert the user request into a brief patient-focused storyboard in English and output only a complete JSON object (no markdown). Use this exact schema: {"title":"Short title","scenes":[{"scene_number":1,"narration":"Brief narration","visual_description":"Brief visual instructions","on_screen_text":"Short caption"}]}. Follow the requested scene count, keep narration concise, and do not add invented citations or treatment details.

Example input:
Create a brief patient-education scene that explains why people with chronic heart failure should receive flu, pneumococcal, and COVID-19 vaccines and how to vaccinate safely while taking anticoagulant medication.
Example output:
{"title": "Vaccines Protect Heart Failure Patients", "scenes": [{"scene_number": 1, "narration": "Patients with chronic heart failure are at higher risk from flu, pneumonia, and COVID; getting vaccinated reduces severe illness and hospital stays, even when on blood thinners.", "visual_description": "Doctor discussing vaccines with patient holding a syringe.", "on_screen_text": "Vaccinate for safety"}]}

This is the historical direct-generation winner. The current app's fixed editing instructions keep its output as plain text in the requested language regardless.
